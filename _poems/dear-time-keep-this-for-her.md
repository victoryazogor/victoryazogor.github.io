*[ Walks towards an empty field ]...cold evening breeze.*

Dear Time, keep this 💌 for HER... 🖋️

MY NAME IS NAVI.  
You might have heard myths, tales and dark sayings of Legends. Well I'm here to to complete the last paragraph of the Book before time seals it away.

In a world of 8 Billion people  
I met a Girl, in the mid hours of my life, you may know HER.

Time holds dear her voice, for its the sound of Love caressing the morning dew.  
In her eyes i Believed in gods existence — fine face like Anansa, i prayed to God and he answered.

Philio-sophia God breathe HER he smiled and said it was Good.

Dear God, is she Human or she's just like you. Will Loving her bring Nephilims once again?  
Oh Lord  for i might be ready to waste the Earth again just to hold her in my arms for a night.

For Good and pleasant Is her sight, her perfume scents better than Myrhh  
Her eyes cuts deep into the soul the rays of a thousand stars.  
A magnificent being in the Milky Way galaxies.

You created an Obsidian and because you wanted to Boast you named HER  
It could have been any other name but no, you choosed " ANGEL💕"

So Lord forgive my mortal mind and this mortal hands of mine, for the things i desire to do to HER.  
Hold HER in this arms of mine and caress HER all through the night.  Lay on this grass and watch the morning star.

Feel her breathe next to mine like I'm inhaling the breathe of life.

Dear Time, before you turn this page, let me tell HER something more. 🖋️

HER, perhaps forever is a promise even the heavens are afraid to make, but if I were given a thousand lives and a thousand chances to begin again, I would still pray that somewhere between creation and eternity, our paths would cross. For what is the beauty of a thousand stars if I cannot tell you how beautifully you shine? What good is a lifetime if I never get to witness the little things that make you, YOU?

Dear God, I ask not that you make her mine by force, nor that you bind her wings to my desires. Only let me be a beautiful chapter in the book of her life, one she would read again without regret. And if Time must eventually seal this book away, let the final ink spell her name. For even if I never get to call her mine, I will forever be grateful that I lived in the same world as HER.
