I think I've exhausted my lines.  
Well, that's a lie.  
And I'm not good at telling lies.

I'd rather lie next to you  
And draw lines brighter than latitudes and meridians,  
'Cause my love for you is like the meridian oceans.

Blue, calm, wild,  
Storms and tornadoes —  
But yes, that's what brings the beauty.

When I leave this life,  
I'll tell my Maker, even though He knows,  
That I met a woman who's strong like a bear.  
It took me a short while to see her imperfections,  
But somehow, I loved her imperfections.

She ain't perfect,  
And I'm not close to being perfect too.  
And that's what makes us a perfect duo.

I'll keep our memories in time...

This writer is lost in his thoughts,  
Do not save me.  
I wish I was better for you,  
But life won its game.

It may hold no meaning,  
But if I'm ever asked to say my last words,  
Then my last words to you would be:

THANK YOU (HOME)
