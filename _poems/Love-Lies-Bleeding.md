LOVE LIES BLEEDING 🩸

(An empty room, gentle wind flowing in through the open windows). \
The stars are aligning tonight. \
I thought Orion and the Pleiades would give me a sign. \
Is love a celestial language and were just second-language speakers \
we only speak with an accent, \
or is it just the way you treat me when you're happy? \
Because your fluency has anguished my soul. \

If love could survive on words alone, why did He give His Son? \
He bled on Calvary for people whose care rises and falls with their moods.\
Maybe real love leaves marks—on the palms,on the ribs—from iron nails, \
and from a tongue that once said, "I love you." \
Love and hurt became synonyms the moment love knew your lips. \

You say love is a choice, not just a feeling— \
then tell me, is this how you've chosen to love me? \
I think love lied bleeding when you lied you loved me; \
