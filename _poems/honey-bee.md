Buzzing... Radio chatter...\
The kettle whooshes.\
It's Monday again.\
The streets are busy,\
yet everything feels empty.\
The weekend's laughter has dissolved,\
and my friends are swallowed by their schedules.

But even Mondays need a hand to hold\
and a heart to console.\
My Sony console can occupy my hands,\
but it cannot entertain the thoughts\
my heart keeps playing,\
those red, purple and white desires.\
My thumb runs laps around the stick,\
a thousand miles going nowhere.\
I need a leap.

Your presence... me encantas.\
I wish you'd pay your rent.\
Perhaps then I'd never have to work,\
since you live here free, in my mind.\
You keep pollinating the quiet corners of me,\
like honeybees returning to a garden\
they have always called home.

I still see you moving through the house,\
touching everything\
without disturbing a thing.\
I bought more extra-large white T-shirts\
because I know how much you love wearing them.\
My halo, te amo.

I hope this new week is as beautiful as you.\
And yes, I'm waiting for the weekend.\
Because somehow,\
without you,\
the weekends are merely weak-ends.

I still hide my feelings around you.\
Not because they're little,\
but because they make me nervous.\
I like you a lot,\
a pre-tense,\
with no pretence.
