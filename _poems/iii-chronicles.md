III CHRONICLES \
​Mary wasn’t perfect, yet God entrusted her with His Son. \
She wasn’t perfect, just favoured enough to carry perfection within her. \
Is perfection an art? \
​Like young Timothy, I’m writing this letter to my future sons. \
​What good is a father who teaches his sons about perfection, \
but never tells them about his own imperfections? \
​Listen. \
There’s a thin line between perfection and imperfection, 
and the difference is "in." \
You might never know which side you’re on. \
​Isn’t life a chessboard,
filled with pawns left to move around \
until they become hanging pieces? \
​Dancing to the echoes of their sins, \
paying for iniquities as next of kin. \
​My mind is an open Bible; \
free verses are my life’s subtitles. |
​Dear son, if you should inherit anything, may it be my wisdom, not my sins.
As a father passes down wisdom to his son’s ear,
listen to my silence. \
​Perhaps perfection is a language,
but why must I lose my voice to speak it?
​My son, do not send Mary away when she comes home bearing a seed.
​For sometimes, life’s miracles are concealed.
​Wasn’t Soponim rejected in her labor, yet she bore Melchizedek, a king?
And Nitzevet bore David in sin.
​Sometimes, sin is concealed.
​My son, even a virgin woman can still wrap serpents around her waist.
If perfection were within our reach,
why would He have had to send His Son?
​My son, listen.
The lips of a harlot are no longer an unknown language,
but beware the lips that paint scriptures around it—
for in my time, a man’s fall comes from the woman within.
​My sons, hold fast unto Mary when you find her,
for Mary is no longer a Jew.
​Her worth is far above dollars and rupees, rare and hard to find; \
in her, a husband’s heart is safe, and he has peace of mind.
​She does him good and never harm.
Like merchant ships, she gathers wisdom from afar;
she buys the field and plants the vine beneath the star. \
She girds her loins with quiet strength and holds the thread; \
her candle never dims by night till all are fed.
So when you see her spindle turn, know grace is near—
the virtues of a Proverbs king live right inside her. \
​Yet, she is not perfect,
but bears favour and grace.
​— Navi.
